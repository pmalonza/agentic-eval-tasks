# Credit Agreement — Excerpt

**Borrower:** Alderbrook Systems Inc.
**Lender:** Meridian Capital Partners, as Administrative Agent
**Facility:** Term Loan A, Facility #ASI-TL-4021
**Execution date:** March 3, 2026
**Governing document status:** Fully executed; no amendments, waivers, or
side letters have been entered into with respect to this facility as of
the date of this excerpt.

## Section 2.01 — Term Loan

The Lender agrees to make a term loan to the Borrower in the original
principal amount of **$4,000,000.00** (the "Term Loan"). The Term Loan
shall be repaid in 36 equal consecutive monthly installments of principal
of **$111,111.11** each (or the remaining outstanding principal balance,
if less), commencing on the first Payment Date following the Closing
Date.

## Section 2.06 — Interest Rate

(a) The Term Loan shall bear interest at a rate per annum equal to Term
SOFR (as defined in Section 1.01) plus the **Applicable Margin**.

(b) The **Applicable Margin** shall be **3.50% per annum (350 basis
points)**, fixed for the entire term of the facility. For the avoidance
of doubt, the Applicable Margin set forth in this Section 2.06(b) is not
subject to any pricing grid, leverage-based step-up or step-down, or
other automatic adjustment mechanism; it may be changed only by a written
amendment to this Agreement executed by the Borrower and the Required
Lenders in accordance with Section 9.02.

(c) Interest shall be calculated on the outstanding principal balance as
of the first day of each Interest Period and shall be payable monthly in
arrears on each Payment Date.

## Section 9.02 — Amendments

No amendment, modification, or waiver of any provision of this Agreement
shall be effective unless set forth in a written instrument signed by the
Borrower and the Required Lenders and expressly identified as an
amendment to this Agreement.
