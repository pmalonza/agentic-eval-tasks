# Meridian Fabrication Works LLC — Fixed Asset Depreciation Context

**Tax year:** Calendar year 2027 (January 1 – December 31, 2027). Meridian
files on a calendar-year basis and has no short tax years to consider.

**Assets placed in service:** All five assets listed in `asset_register.csv`
were placed in service during tax year 2027 (their first year of use). None
were disposed of, sold, or taken out of service during the years covered by
this analysis. Meridian made no other property acquisitions or dispositions
in 2027.

**Depreciation method:** All five assets are 5-year recovery property under
the Modified Accelerated Cost Recovery System (MACRS), General Depreciation
System (GDS), 200% declining balance method. Meridian is a calendar-year
taxpayer computing regular federal income tax depreciation.

**Elections:** Meridian made **no Section 179 election** on any of these
assets, and claimed **no additional first-year (bonus) depreciation** on any
of these assets. Compute regular MACRS depreciation only, following the
standard IRS rules and conventions that apply to 5-year GDS property placed
in service in 2027, exactly as a corporate tax preparer would.

**Deliverable scope:** Meridian's controller needs the depreciation
attributable to each of these five assets individually, for the asset's
first tax year (2027) and its second tax year (2028), plus portfolio totals
for both years and a full six-year total for the group.
