# Vehicle Service Contract — Terms

**Contract:** VSC-88214
**Vehicle:** New vehicle, 0 miles at the time of sale (VSC sold at the
same time as the vehicle purchase)
**VSC gross premium (price paid by customer):** $1,800.00

## Manufacturer's warranty (already in effect from the vehicle
manufacturer, separate from this service contract)

- Term: 36 months
- Term: 30,000 miles
- Terminates at whichever of the above is reached first, measured from
  the date of the vehicle's original purchase (same date as the VSC
  sale, since the VSC was sold with a new vehicle at 0 miles).

## VSC (this service contract)

- Term: 60 months
- Term: 75,000 miles
- Terminates at whichever of the above is reached first, measured from
  the date of sale.

## Assumed average mileage

For purposes of this analysis, assume the vehicle is driven a flat
**1,000 miles per month** for the life of the contract (the firm's
standard planning assumption for this vehicle segment; do not use a
probabilistic mileage distribution for this analysis).
