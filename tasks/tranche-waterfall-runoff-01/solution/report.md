# Northfield Instruments LLC — Three-Tranche Facility Runoff (#TW-2027-311)

## 1. Executive summary

The $3,300,000 facility (Tranche A Senior $2,000,000, Tranche B Mezzanine
$800,000, Tranche C Subordinated $500,000) runs off in strict payment
priority order over the 24-month window:

- **Tranche A (Senior) is fully repaid in Month 12**, driven by its
  $83,333.33 scheduled amortization plus first-priority cash sweeps in
  Months 1-11.
- **Tranche B (Mezzanine) is fully repaid in Month 15** — three months
  after Tranche A, once sweep proceeds begin flowing to it.
- **Tranche C (Subordinated) converts from PIK to cash-pay in Month 16**
  (the first month both A and B show a zero balance as of month-start) and
  is **fully repaid in Month 18**.
- The interest-rate covenant **triggers once, in Month 7** (Free Cash Flow
  Before Debt Service of -$30,000). Two distinct, differently-durationed
  effects follow, both effective Month 8: Tranche A's rate **permanently**
  steps up from 8.00% to 9.00%, while Tranche B's interest is forced into
  PIK for **Month 8 only** — a one-month liquidity relief valve, not a
  permanent change.
- **Total cash interest paid:** Tranche A $77,531.19; Tranche B $93,029.97;
  Tranche C $11,936.54 (post-conversion only). **Total PIK'd interest:**
  Tranche B $7,333.33 (the single forced-PIK month); Tranche C $95,020.07
  (all months before conversion).
- **Lowest cash balance:** $103,174.06, in Month 7 — the same month the
  covenant triggers.
- **Ending cash balance (Month 24):** $2,925,148.90, with all three
  tranches fully retired since Month 18.

## 3. The asymmetric covenant: one trigger, two different durations

Month 7 is the only month with negative Free Cash Flow Before Debt Service
(-$30,000). Per `loan_terms.md`, this triggers two effects simultaneously
effective Month 8, but with different lifespans:

- **Tranche A's rate permanently steps up** to 9.00% starting Month 8 and
  stays there through Month 12 (when A is retired) — this never reverts,
  even though every month from 8 onward is cash-flow positive.
- **Tranche B's interest is force-PIK'd for Month 8 only.** Month 8's
  $7,333.33 of Tranche B interest is capitalized into its balance
  regardless of cash sufficiency (raising B's balance from $800,000.00 to
  $807,333.33). Starting Month 9, Tranche B's ordinary cash-sufficiency
  test resumes — Month 9 pays $7,400.56 in cash, confirming the forced-PIK
  effect did not carry forward. Applying the forced PIK permanently
  instead of for one month would be the most tempting misreading here,
  since it mirrors Tranche A's genuinely-permanent change — but the two
  effects are explicitly different durations in `loan_terms.md`.

## 4. Waterfall priority and the Tranche C conversion gate

The sweep strictly follows payment priority: Months 1-11 sweep entirely to
Tranche A (the only tranche above zero); Month 12 onward sweeps entirely to
Tranche B once A hits zero; Month 16 onward sweeps entirely to Tranche C
once both A and B hit zero **and** Tranche C has converted to cash-pay.

Tranche C's conversion is gated on **both** A and B reaching zero, not
either alone. Tranche A alone reaches zero in Month 12, but Tranche C
continues accruing PIK interest for four more months (12-15) because
Tranche B is still outstanding — only in Month 16, the first month
following B's Month-15 payoff, does Tranche C convert to cash-pay. A
reader who assumes conversion happens as soon as the *senior-most* tranche
clears (i.e., at Month 12, when only Tranche A is retired) would compute a
materially different — and wrong — split between Tranche C's PIK and cash
interest totals, and a wrong conversion month.

One further subtlety: in Month 15, the raw computed sweep ($231,652.52)
slightly exceeds Tranche B's remaining balance at that point ($231,199.53).
Per `loan_terms.md`, the sweep is capped at B's balance and the $452.99
excess is **not** redirected to Tranche C that same month (Tranche C has
not yet converted in Month 15 regardless) — it simply remains in cash,
carried forward to Month 16.

## 5. Liquidity risk note

The lowest cash balance reached at any point is **$103,174.06, in Month
7** — the direct result of that month's -$30,000 free cash flow landing in
the same month as Tranche A's and Tranche B's combined debt service, with
no sweep occurring that month (cash never cleared the $150,000 sweep
threshold). The facility recovers immediately in Month 8 without missing
any payment.

rule.

## Assumptions and limitations

- Tranche C's conversion test uses tranche balances **as of the start of
  the month** (i.e., after the prior month's activity) — Month 15 is the
  month Tranche B's balance reaches zero, so Month 16 is the first month
  Tranche C sees both balances at zero at month-start and converts.
- The one-time forced-PIK override for Tranche B applies only in the
  single month immediately following the covenant trigger (Month 8); no
  other month is affected by it, per the explicit "one-time... not a
  permanent change" language in `loan_terms.md`.
- Consistent with the stated rule, a sweep amount larger than the current
  priority recipient's balance is capped at that balance; the excess is
  not applied to the next tranche in the same month.
